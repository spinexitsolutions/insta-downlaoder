{{ site.name }} is a free online tool that lets you save public Instagram content: Reels, videos, IGTV, photos, carousel posts, stories and profile pictures. You can use it from any browser on your phone or computer, without installing an app or logging in.

Our mission

Instagram is full of useful content: recipes, tutorials, workouts, travel ideas and memories. But the app doesn't let you keep it for offline viewing. We built {{ site.name }} to make saving that content for personal use simple, fast and private.

What we believe in

  • Simplicity: paste a link, get your file. No sign-ups, no clutter.
  • Privacy: we don't ask for your Instagram login and we don't keep copies of what you download.
  • Respect for creators: we only support public content and we act quickly on copyright requests.
